About

Adaptive by nature.
Disciplined by design.

Protean's proprietary agentic AI pattern recognition algorithm generates a continuously revised opportunity set for risk-taking while human oversight ensures tactical decision-making, portfolio balance, and adaptability to evolving market conditions and regime changes.

Our Flagship Managed Convexity Strategy seeks to maximize overall portfolio convexity and exposure to our AI-generated alphas through daily active management while minimizing correlation to our benchmark.

Strategy

Flagship Managed Convexity

The strategy leverages Protean's agentic AI pattern recognition signals — grounded in Fractional Brownian Motion modeling — to identify geometric inflection points across global markets and dynamically manage opportunistic and uncorrelated exposures.

Portfolio construction emphasizes bespoke, mixed-duration convexity vehicles, with a systematic focus on net vega reduction — built to endure the full spectrum of market regimes.

Derivative Instrumentation

Debit spreads serve as the primary structure, offering inherent vega mitigation. Shorter-duration opportunities may utilize outright puts or calls, while orthogonal positions may employ covered credit spreads for additional net vega reduction.
